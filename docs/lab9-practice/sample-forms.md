# Lab 9 — PRACTICE sample forms, Set A: version A (concise report) — fictional

> **PRACTICE ONLY — FICTIONAL PARTICIPANTS S1–S5. NOT TEST DATA. Do not copy into the Lab 9 report.**
> Set A = five people who used **version A** (concise report, port 5173) for T1. Set B (version B, B1–B5) is in
> `sample-forms-version-b.md`, with the A vs B comparison at its end.
> Purpose: rehearse filling, scoring and summarising the real participant forms. Results are deliberately mixed
> (one target missed) so the "met / not met / pending" write-up can be practised too.

Assumptions used for the samples (check against your build before the real tests):

- T1 report shows heart rate **112 bpm**, normal **72–96**, status **Above range**, advice: nurse check / see a clinician.
- T2 Study Hub records **four signals: ECG, EMG, rPPG, thermal**; full data needs **request + data use agreement**.
- Expert times (makers' best of 3): **T1 = 70 s, T2 = 45 s** → time targets ≤ 140 s and ≤ 90 s.

Marking guide: ✓ = 1 · ~ = 0.5 · × = 0. SUS: odd items (score − 1), even items (5 − score), sum × 2.5.
