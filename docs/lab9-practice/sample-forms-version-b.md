| Targets met (T1) | 3 of 4 (errors ❌) | 3 of 4 (time ❌) | — |

Reading it: version B is understood better and causes fewer errors, but takes longer — people read the explanations
and scroll the data. Two Set B users found the bottom charts confusing or too long (B3, B5). A reasonable decision:
**keep B's first screen (range bars + one-line explanations) and drop or collapse the "All your data" section**, which
would keep the comprehension gain and cut the time. With 5 people per group this is indicative only.

T2 is the same Study Hub in both sets; its differences are just different people, not the A/B change.
