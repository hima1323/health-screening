# Triage knowledge text (DRAFT: must be reviewed and approved by a clinician before use)

The explainer and the follow-up Q&A may use ONLY this text and the session evidence record. Keep each section short and
plain. No diagnoses, no medication or dosing advice. Sections are retrieved by their key (the `##` heading).
Sections from the triage starter kit; the last five are added for the follow-up questions the kiosk gets most.
`{emergency}` and `{crisis}` are filled from contacts.yaml by code.

## heart_rate_high
A resting heart rate above about 100 beats per minute is faster than typical. Common harmless reasons include caffeine,
recent activity, stress, dehydration or a mild illness. If it stays high when you are rested, or if you also have chest
pain, dizziness, fainting or breathlessness, a health professional should check it.

## heart_rate_low
A resting heart rate below about 60 beats per minute can be normal in people who train regularly. If you feel dizzy,
faint or unusually tired, a health professional should check it.

## breathing_fast
Breathing faster than about 20 breaths per minute at rest is more than typical. Talking, anxiety or recent activity can
raise it. If you feel short of breath, seek help promptly.

## temperature_high
A raised temperature can be a sign of infection. Camera temperature readings are estimates; a thermometer reading is more
accurate. If you feel unwell with a fever, or it lasts more than a few days, see a doctor.

## mood_screen_positive
Your answers suggest you may have been feeling low or anxious recently. Talking to a doctor or a mental health
professional can help. This check cannot diagnose any condition.

## crisis
If you are thinking about harming yourself, please contact {crisis} or {emergency} now, or speak to someone you trust.

## measurement_limits
This kiosk estimates heart rate, breathing and temperature with cameras. It cannot measure blood pressure or oxygen
level unless the extra devices are used, and it cannot diagnose conditions.

## out_of_scope_questions
I can't answer questions about medicines, doses or specific illnesses. Please ask a doctor or pharmacist.

## why_repeated
A reading is taken twice so that a single unusual value, for example from moving or talking, does not decide the
result. If a reading is close to the limit or the two readings disagree, the check asks you to rest and measures again.

## result_urgent
An urgent result means something in your answers or readings needs prompt attention. Go to an emergency department or
call {emergency}. Do not wait to see if it passes.

## result_see_doctor_soon
This result means a doctor should look at this in the next few days. It is not an emergency. If you start to feel much
worse, get help sooner.

## result_self_care
No warning signs were found by this check. That does not rule out every problem: if you feel unwell, see a doctor anyway.

## result_not_suitable
This check is designed for adults aged 18 to 65 who are not pregnant, because its limits were set for that group. Please
ask a doctor, nurse or pharmacist instead.

## off_topic
I can only answer questions about this check and your result. For anything else, please ask a doctor, nurse or
pharmacist.

## contacts
In an emergency call {emergency}. If you need to talk to someone about how you feel, call {crisis}.

## data_privacy
Your answers and readings are stored without your name, only to check how well this kiosk works. The camera
recording is used for the measurement and is not kept.
